# AI in Health Care: Pharmacology

Academic research poster exploring the role of Artificial Intelligence in modern pharmacology and healthcare systems.

## Overview

This academic poster investigates how Artificial Intelligence is transforming pharmacology through machine learning, predictive analytics, and intelligent healthcare systems.

The work explores how AI supports drug discovery, clinical trials, precision dosing, and pharmacovigilance while also discussing ethical and legal challenges.

---

## Topics Covered

- Artificial Intelligence in healthcare
- Drug discovery applications
- Preclinical testing
- Clinical trial optimization
- Precision dosing
- Pharmacovigilance
- Drug repurposing
- Ethical and legal considerations
- AI limitations and challenges

---

## Case Study

The poster includes a case study examining machine learning based prediction techniques in pharmacology and personalized medicine.

---

## Key Discussion Areas

### Benefits

- Faster drug discovery
- Reduced costs
- Improved prediction accuracy
- Personalized treatment

### Limitations

- Lack of transparency
- Biased datasets
- Regulatory challenges
- Privacy concerns
